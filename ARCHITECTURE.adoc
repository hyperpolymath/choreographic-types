= Architecture — choreographic-types
:toc: preamble
:toc-title: Contents

This repository is a checked Agda development plus the documents that say,
honestly, what it does and does not prove. There is no runtime, no package
and no service: the "architecture" is the shape of the proof development,
the siblings it imports, and the gate that checks it.

== Layout

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Path | What lives there

| `src/Choreographic/`
| The development. `Party`, `Global` (graded global types, cuts),
  `Local`, `Projection` (plain merge, cut projection, `Consistent`), `KCut`
  (K-CUT-LOSS), `Kripke` and `Bound` (K-CUT-WARRANT in bound form),
  `Transport` (the transport component), `Swap` and `Operational` (the
  two-thread diamond), `Instances` (the discharges at `Loss`, `MaxPlus`
  and `HWM`), `Base/RoleGraded` (the echo-types base case recovered) and
  `Base/EchoAdditive` (echo-types' ℕ ∪ {∞} grade as a `ResourceAlgebra`).
  `All.agda` is the root that checks everything builtins-only.

| `tests/reject/`
| Expected rejections. Each file must fail at its `invalid` declaration
  with a type mismatch, so that every side-condition is seen to be
  load-bearing.

| `tests/minimal-laws/`
| A deliberately impoverished `ResourceAlgebra.Core` used to certify which
  algebra laws K-CUT-LOSS depends on.

| `scripts/`
| The gate (`fetch-siblings.sh`, `check.sh`, `minimal-laws.sh`) and the
  documentation integrity check (`check-repository.mjs`), which also binds
  every name the ledger cites to its definition and the definition's comment
  back to the row.

| `applications/`
| Application notes and a postulated RapidNJ target; not part of the gate
  and not a proof.

| `docs/`
| The pre-registration record and its dated addenda, the Actions policy
  note, and cross-repository issue drafts.

| `.github/workflows/`
| `proofs.yml` (the Agda gate), `documentation-integrity.yml`, the estate's
  shared scanners, and `actions.lock`.

| `PROOF-STATUS.adoc`
| The ledger: every claim with PROVEN / TESTED / ASSUMED / DESIGNED / OPEN
  and the module that discharges it.
|===

== Layers

. *Resource algebra* — imported from the sibling `resource-algebra` at a
  pinned commit: the parametric `ResourceAlgebra` (alternatives ⊕, sequence
  ⊗, the induced order ≼) with its dioid, ordered and annihilating layers,
  and the `Loss`, `MaxPlus` and `HWM` instances.
. *Global and local types, projection, cuts* — `src/Choreographic/Global`,
  `Local`, `Projection`: the finite, binary-choice, plain-merge fragment,
  graded over any resource algebra.
. *The keystone* — `KCut` proves K-CUT-LOSS as an equality; `Kripke`,
  `Bound` and `Transport` prove K-CUT-WARRANT in bound form and its
  transport component, instantiating `epistemic-types`' `Warrant`,
  `SoundWarrant` and `Access.Preorder` rather than imitating them.
. *Asynchrony* — `Async` adds cuts whose messages and selections are
  delivered or in flight, and proves K-CUT-LOSS for them; `AsyncKnowledge`
  is the Kripke frame over them (the receiver of a protocol's first message
  knows it was sent, its sender does not know it was received while the
  receiver has done nothing since); `AsyncBound` is the
  bound against the sent-so-far grade.
. *Instances and applications* — `Instances` discharges the keystone at
  the three estate algebras and computes a worked protocol; `Swap` and
  `Operational` read the RapidNJ two-thread diamond at the type level;
  `Base/EchoAdditive` ties echo-types' own grade to `Loss`.

== Dependencies and pins

`scripts/fetch-siblings.sh` fetches exactly one commit of each sibling
(`resource-algebra`, `epistemic-types`, `echo-types`), by full SHA, and
fails on a mismatch. The siblings are passed as explicit include paths
under `--no-libraries`; nothing outside the pins is read. Only
`Base/EchoAdditive` needs the Agda standard library, through echo-types, so
it is checked as its own root and `All.agda` stays builtins-only.

== The gate

`.github/workflows/proofs.yml` runs in a digest-pinned Debian 13 container
with the distribution's `agda-bin=2.6.4.3-1+b2` and `agda-stdlib=2.1-4`,
asserts the prover version, fetches the siblings, and runs `scripts/check.sh`
and `scripts/minimal-laws.sh` with
`--safe --without-K --no-libraries --ignore-interfaces --double-check -W error`.
The documentation integrity workflow is structural only and never evidence
that anything is proved.

== Boundaries

* A cut is a consistent frontier of a causal order, not a proof-theoretic
  cut.
* Lean 4 developments (tropical-types) are provenance, never imports: the
  Lean→Agda bridge for `MaxPlus` is resource-algebra's open obligation M-2.
* Implementation belongs to `typell`; this repository proves, it does not
  run.
