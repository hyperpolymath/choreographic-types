Cross-reference glossary for link:README.adoc[README.adoc], link:EXPLAINME.adoc[EXPLAINME.adoc], and the `choreographic-types` wiki. Terms are classified as **standard** (existing concept used without modification), **rephrased** (standard concept with project-specific vocabulary), or **project-specific** (originating in this project).

== Core concepts

[[global-type]]
Global type (G)::
  A choreographic specification of a multiparty interaction, read here as a partial causal order of communication events.
  *Classification:* **rephrased**. Global types are standard in multiparty session types (Honda–Yoshida–Carbone). Reading them explicitly as partial causal orders with antichain cuts is the project-specific emphasis.
  *See also:* <<cut>>, <<endpoint-projection>>

[[endpoint-projection]]
Endpoint projection::
  The mapping from a global type `G` to the local type at each participant. Standard in choreographic programming.
  *Classification:* **standard**.
  *See also:* <<k-cut>>

[[cut]]
Cut (consistent frontier)::
  An antichain across the partial causal order of a global type: a set of events such that no two are causally related. Represents a consistent snapshot of the distributed interaction.
  *Classification:* **rephrased**. Antichains and consistent cuts are standard in event structures and distributed systems. Applying them as the cut structure for session-type grading is project-specific.
  *See also:* <<k-cut>>

== Grading

[[echo-loss-grade]]
Echo loss-grade::
  The grade attached to a choreographic edge, measuring the structured information loss at that interaction. Drawn from the `ℕ ∪ {∞}` loss-dioid in `echo-types`.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (composition of echo-types vocabulary with choreographic types).

[[epistemic-standpoint-warrant]]
Epistemic standpoint-warrant::
  The grade attached to a choreographic edge, measuring the evidence required to authorise that interaction. Drawn from `epistemic-types`.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (composition of epistemic-types vocabulary with choreographic types).

[[loss-modality]]
Loss modality (∇ / △)::
  ∇ contingent: loss may occur at this cut. △ non-contingent: loss is impossible. Indexed by an ordinal loss value.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

== The keystone

[[k-cut]]
K-CUT::
  The conjecture that grading and transport commute with endpoint projection across a consistent frontier (cut). Splits into <<k-cut-loss>> and <<k-cut-warrant>>.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (conjecture).
  *Status:* **OPEN**.

[[k-cut-loss]]
K-CUT-LOSS::
  The fragment of K-CUT concerning echo loss-grades. Holds as an *equality*: the grade of the global cut equals the grade computed locally at the endpoint, because loss is type-determined.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (conjecture).
  *Status:* **OPEN** (base case proved in `echo-types`).

[[k-cut-warrant]]
K-CUT-WARRANT::
  The fragment of K-CUT concerning epistemic warrants. Holds only as a *bound* under a `SoundWarrant` side-condition: the type upper-bounds discoverability but cannot determine it.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (conjecture).
  *Status:* **OPEN**.

== Patterns

[[port-and-reprove]]
Port-and-reprove pattern::
  Re-proving a result in-site rather than importing across kernels or language boundaries. Ensures self-containment at the cost of manual synchronisation.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (estate pattern). Precedent: `typed-wasm/…/Tropical.idr`.
