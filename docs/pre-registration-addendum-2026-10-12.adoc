* K-CUT-LOSS: OPEN → PROVEN (fragment: finite, binary choice, plain merge;
  parametric in `ResourceAlgebra`; three discharges).

  (`Global`, `Local`, `proj`, `Cut`, `Consistent`).
* "Cross-prover bridge": dissolved for loss — resource-algebra is Agda, same
  kernel, imported not re-proved. The Lean bridge obligation lives in
  resource-algebra (M-2), not here.

  is resolved by construction: a grade names its algebra through the
  instance; `grade↾` is defined once over the record.

* K-CUT-WARRANT, bound form: OPEN → PROVEN (same fragment). The model is a
  Kripke frame over consistent cuts with sameness-of-view accessibility;
  epistemic-types' `SoundWarrant` is instantiated, not imitated. The
  pre-registered statement below (warrant as a second grade function) was
  replaced before any proof by the knowledge formulation, because the
  frame makes "the type cannot determine it" a theorem rather than a remark.

* K-CUT-WARRANT, transport: OPEN → PROVEN (same fragment). Delivered as
  pre-registered, with one correction made before proving: the statement
  "r knows the global cut extends the send" is false under plain merge
  when the send sits inside a choice r is outside of (the other branch
  carries an identical local event), so the theorem is about the *local*
  frontier — which is exactly what r can know. `AccessibleModality` needed
  a world index to be instantiable (T-6).
