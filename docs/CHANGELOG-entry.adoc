== 2026-11-01 — four research tasks (echo projection, warrant claim, grading semantics, Independent₂)

* `src/Choreographic/Base/EchoProjection.agda`: the choreographic analog
  of echo-types' `choreo-grade-commute`.  Two 2D families indexed by Party
  and EGrade (grade-indexed and local-type-indexed), with grade degradation
  and party transport.  The commuting square holds by `refl` in every case
  (EP-1, EP-2), but the party transport requires identical restricted grades
  or identical projections as a hypothesis.  In the two-event case (EP-3),
  the transport exists only between parties with equal values, making it
  the identity; the square is trivial in ALL cases.  The echo-types square
  has a non-trivial case because `client-to-server` maps between different
  types; the choreographic analog has no such map.  This is recorded as the
  result: the B-5 open item (role transport) cannot be closed by this route.
* `src/Choreographic/WarrantClaim.agda`: precise K-CUT-WARRANT theorem
  statement with every hypothesis and side condition explicit (WC-1 to
  WC-5).  Three components: grade equality warrant (C1, proven from
  K-CUT-LOSS and frame factivity), grade bound warrant (C2, proven with
  grade-ordering hypothesis H4), data warrant (C3, conditional on
  receiver-local side condition H5).  The gap between type-determined
  and data-dependent warrants is documented precisely.  epistemic-types'
  `SoundWarrant` interface is used as-is; no assumed warrant is turned
  into a proved fact.
* `src/Choreographic/GradingSemantics.agda`: grading semantics for bounds
  under interaction, using tropical-types (Lean 4, read-only provenance)
  for the algebraic vocabulary.  Keeps grade, measure, and index distinct.
  Derives one-step bound propagation (GS-1, GS-2), two-step composition
  and projection theorem (GS-3).  Negative result (GS-4): unrestricted
  measure is NOT compositional under reordering for non-commutative ⊗;
  recorded as consequence of A-1 and A-3.  No postulates; no cross-prover
  bridge claimed.
