== Fragment formalised (scope, stated before the claims)

Finite graded global types with message and *binary* choice; endpoint
projection with *plain merge* (a non-participant's two branches must
project identically); cuts as frontiers (`stop` / `cont-*`); consistent
cuts = plain merge still holds on the truncated branches. Grades are drawn
from any `ResourceAlgebra`. *Not* in the fragment: recursion (μ-types),
full merge, delegation, asynchrony/queues, n-ary choice (a fold of binary),
delegation, and data-dependent warrants (the frame carries only what the
type determines). Both components of K-CUT-WARRANT — bound and transport
— are in, for this fragment.

== Open (honest — NOT proved)

* *Data-dependent warrants.* The frame carries only what the type
  determines; warrants about payloads are outside it, and there
  `SoundWarrant` stays an assumption — with a precise scope (W-2, T-2).
* *Asynchrony.* With queues, T-2's sender half (`sender-knows`) is expected
  to fail: a send without receipt is a world the sender cannot distinguish
  from one with receipt. Pre-registered as the falsifier that separates
  synchronous from asynchronous K-CUT.
* *Recursion.* μ-types change `Cut` (a frontier through an unfolding) and need
  a coinductive or fuel-indexed grade. Not attempted.
* *Full merge.* Only plain merge. Full merge (branches may differ in labels
  the non-participant later distinguishes) would weaken `Merge`; K-3's
  idempotence step is exactly what would need to generalise.
* *Role transport* (B-5).
* *Operational `step` as a function.* Kept relational; a functional step on
  the quotient needs decidable grade equality or a canonical-form theorem
  for swap-equivalence. Not attempted.
* *Sibling pins.* resource-algebra at b2c9f1f6bc7ed7cc83c7573014cdf88d49d2a881, epistemic-types at 495db9c
  (`scripts/fetch-siblings.sh`); `proofs.yml` ran green on the Debian 2.6.4.3 pin before #23 merged.

== What W-4 says about the keystone

The bound's direction is not a convention: it is the S-3 finding of
resource-algebra (opposite-running orders) surfacing in the keystone. In HWM
and MaxPlus p's view is a *lower* bound on the global grade; in Loss it is
an *upper* bound in ≼, i.e. numerically p's view accounts for no more loss
than the global total. Any K-CUT-WARRANT statement must say which.

== What K-4 says about the keystone

That K-CUT-LOSS survives with only idempotence and a left unit means the
*equality* half of the keystone is algebraically weak: it holds for
non-commutative, non-associative alternatives. The structure the family
cares about — order, monotone sequencing, the opposite-running orders of
Loss and MaxPlus — is first exercised by the *bound* half, K-CUT-WARRANT.
That is where the next proof effort belongs.
